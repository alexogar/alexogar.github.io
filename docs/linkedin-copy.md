## Suggested LinkedIn post — Figma and design systems

AI makes it faster to generate an interface. That doesn't make it easier to ship a product that feels coherent, accessible and reliable.

I enjoyed joining a conversation with colleagues from Figma and Deutsche Bank in Berlin about AI, design systems and design-to-code.

One point I keep coming back to: if a product uses a shared button, it needs to remain the same button everywhere — not merely look similar in one generated screen. Design systems are becoming an important source of constraints and context for AI-assisted engineering.

The exciting part isn't generating more UI. It's helping engineers build better experiences without giving up the consistency and control users depend on.

Thanks to everyone who organized and joined the discussion.
